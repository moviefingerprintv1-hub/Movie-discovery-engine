# Movie Discovery Engine

A personalized movie and TV discovery engine designed to find what someone actually feels like watching.

## Core Idea

Instead of searching only by genre, title or rating, users can describe the experience they want in natural language.

Searches can include:

- Current and desired emotion
- Atmosphere and mood
- Visual aesthetic
- Architecture
- Technology
- Clothing
- Era
- Location and setting
- Actors and filmmakers
- Story characteristics
- Pacing
- Content preferences
- Traditional movie/TV filters

## Personalization

Users will have a Movie DNA profile based on their preferences and viewing feedback.

The current search always takes priority over the user's historical preferences.

## Recommendation Modes

- Precision
- Know Me
- Explore
- Surprise Me

## Result Modes

- Simple
- Balanced
- Deep Dive

## V1

V1 will support both movies and TV shows.

Initial test catalogue:
- Approximately 750 movies
- Approximately 250 TV series
- Primarily English-language / Western content

The first prototype will begin with a much smaller test catalogue before scaling to 1,000 titles.

## Benchmark Search #1

> I want a futuristic movie set in a world where the technology,
> architecture and overall design look like the 1960s. It must have
> been made after 1995. I want it to make me feel like I'm a little
> kid who has been introduced to Coca-Cola for the first time and
> it's a completely new experience.

The system should understand the emotional meaning of this description,
not interpret Coca-Cola as a literal movie requirement.
Personalized movie and TV discovery engine based on emotion, atmosphere, aesthetics, setting and viewing preferences.
